My first paper was submitted to the arXiv over the summer. We looked at the effects of AGN feedback on galaxy cluster environments. We ran a series of hydrodynamic simulations with varying AGN jet feedback implementations to test the effects on both galaxy properties and the large scale gas distribution. We found that across all tests we pursued, the strongest feedback runs were in best alignment with observations. In addition, we identify hot gas outside of clusters towards higher redshift as a strong signature of AGN feedback.

You can read more [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.13132).
